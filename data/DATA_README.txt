EcoMetric starter datasets

IMPORTANT:
- environmental_operations.csv and sustainability_targets.csv are SIMULATED portfolio-project data.
- Emissions in environmental_operations.csv use illustrative factors (electricity: 0.70 kg CO2e/kWh; fuel: 2.68 kg CO2e/unit). These are not official reporting factors and should not be used for real corporate disclosure.
- co2_reference.csv is a schema/template only. Populate it with public data from Our World in Data:
  https://ourworldindata.org/co2-and-greenhouse-gas-emissions
- Record the source URL, units, retrieval date, and any transformations in project documentation.
